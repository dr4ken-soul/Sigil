# COPY_GUIDELINES.md: Sigil

## Punctuation

A full stop ends a thought, a comma carries one on. Decide which before writing, not after.
The failure mode is a run of short sentences standing in for one sentence. Write "Nothing was changed, so you can try again." not "Nothing was changed. You can try again."
Do not join independent clauses with a bare comma.

## Labels and chips

Labels, eyebrows, chips and stats carry no trailing punctuation.
Helper sentences get exactly one full stop.
Status confirmations such as "Connected." and "Saved." get one full stop.
The glass may use fragments where the thought is complete, for example "Unlimited allowance."

## Voice

Clinical terse, British English. No em dashes anywhere. Short direct sentences.
No filler words: seamlessly, powerful, cutting-edge, unlock, instant, game-changing.
No lorem ipsum. Every headline and label in FRONTEND_SPEC.md is final text.
No financial advice. Flags are facts about a transaction, not recommendations.

## Audit scope

Every screen, component and state, audited at the source string. No em dash in any interface string.

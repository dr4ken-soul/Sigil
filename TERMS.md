# Sigil Terms of Use

Sigil is a demonstration build for the Heads Up: Even Realities Hackathon. By using it you accept these terms.

## Safety

- Sigil explains transactions. It does not give financial advice
- Every demo runs on testnet or read-only data. Do not use it with real funds
- Sigil never holds funds, private keys or mnemonics
- A verdict is not a guarantee. Verify the transaction with your own wallet before signing anything of value

## Availability

This is a hackathon build. It may change, pause or stop without notice. Testnet integrations can lag or fail.

## Acceptable use

Do not use Sigil to automate approvals, to submit malicious requests, or to impersonate another user. Do not reverse engineer or redistribute the package outside the hackathon context.
